Do you remember when we used to play on tiny handheld consoles and feel completely amazed by what such limited hardware could achieve?

The Game Boy Advance had a 32-bit processor running at only 16.8 MHz, just 288 KB of working memory, and a tiny 240-by-160-pixel screen. Yet developers somehow managed to create entire worlds that could fit in the palm of your hand.

I played Grand Theft Auto on the Game Boy Advance when I was a kid, and despite the small screen and simple graphics, it felt like stepping into another dimension—a place where you could explore, cause chaos, and feel as though anything was possible.

That sense of freedom and imagination is what made these games so special.
